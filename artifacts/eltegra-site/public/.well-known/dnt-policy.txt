Auditee DNT Policy (EFF DNT Policy 1.0)
========================================

Auditee respects the W3C "Do Not Track" (DNT) browser signal as
defined by the Electronic Frontier Foundation's DNT Policy v1.0
(https://www.eff.org/dnt-policy).

Effective: 2025-01-01
Site: https://auditee.site

When a visitor sends DNT: 1 (or the equivalent Sec-GPC: 1 / Global
Privacy Control signal):

1. We do not log their IP address beyond what is required to serve
   the request, and we discard any retained log within 10 days.
2. We do not set, read or share any third-party tracking cookie or
   identifier for the purpose of cross-site profiling.
3. We do not pass identifying information to advertising networks,
   analytics processors, or fingerprinting services.
4. First-party essential cookies (session, CSRF, theme, preferences)
   are still set so the site works.
5. Aggregate, de-identified statistics may still be computed for
   internal capacity planning, provided no record can be re-linked
   to an individual visitor.
6. We honor opt-out via the Global Privacy Control header
   (Sec-GPC: 1) identically to DNT: 1.

Compliance & contact: privacy@auditee.site
Full privacy policy: https://auditee.site/privacy-policy
